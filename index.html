Privacy Policy for Forge

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Application Version: 1.0.0 • Package: com.nanda.forge
Summary: Forge is a local-first application. We do not sell your personal data, embed advertising trackers, or transfer your journal entries or task data to external servers. Forge is completely offline and free until it reaches v1.2.0.

1. Local-First Data Storage

All tasks, habits, commitments, goals, and daily journal reflections are stored exclusively in a secure SQLite database located within Forge's private sandbox on your Android device. Your data is protected by your device's native hardware encryption.

2. Sensitive Android Permissions

To deliver automated focus protection and screen-time insights, Forge utilizes specific Android platform capabilities:

  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE):
    Used exclusively for the optional Focus Protection feature during active deep work blocks. It observes the foreground package to warn you if a distracting app is opened.
    Forge NEVER reads keystrokes, personal chats, passwords, or credit cards. All processing is strictly ephemeral and stays on-device.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS):
    Reads total minutes spent in foreground applications to display your daily screen-time breakdown and compute your Focus Alignment Score.
  • Package Visibility (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES):
    Resolves installed application display names (e.g., "YouTube" instead of internal package strings) to allow clean productivity categorization.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):
    Sends scheduled focus timer alerts and daily review check-in reminders locally.

3. Zero Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

Forge does NOT integrate commercial behavioral tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or data broker tools. We have zero interest in monetizing your personal habits or screen time.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You maintain 100% control over your information. You can permanently erase all data at any moment by clearing the application cache and storage via Android Settings → Apps → Forge → Storage → Clear Storage, or simply by uninstalling Forge.
